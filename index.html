Roshani, light the diya.

Every light in this sky is for you.

Keep going, there are five more lights

Five small lights, five wishes

Tap each diya to light it.

Dear Roshani,

Diwali is the festival where we light a tiny flame and watch the whole dark night give way. That is the closest way I can describe what you did for me.

Before you, my days were just days. Now there is a someone to tell the small news to, a smile I look for first, and a reason that every ordinary evening feels a little brighter.

I made this page the way you light a diya: with care, a bit of patience, and hope that it makes you smile. It is small, but every spark in it is meant for you.

May this Diwali fill your home with warmth, your heart with peace, and your year with everything you have been quietly wishing for. And may I be there for all of it.

Always yours, Ajay
